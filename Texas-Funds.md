# Key State Balances & Revenues (As of End of FY 2026)

| Category | Amount | What It Covers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2026–2027 Biennial Budget (All Funds)** | **~$338 billion** | Total approved spending across two years (Sept. 2025 – Aug. 2027), including state taxes and federal funds. |
| **FY 2026 Annual Revenue** | **$188.9 billion** | Total net cash brought in during FY 2026 (including $88.2B in state taxes and $61.1B in federal funds). |
| **General Revenue Cash Balance** | **$30.4 billion** | Cash on hand in the main operating account at the end of FY 2026 (down $11B from FY 2025 due to property tax relief and energy fund transfers). |
| **Special Revenue Funds** | **$28.5 billion** | Cash held in dedicated accounts earmarked for specific uses like highways and infrastructure. |
| **Rainy Day Fund (ESF)** | **$27.5 billion** | Emergency savings reserve ($5.8B cash and $21.7B investments), sitting above its $26.5B constitutional cap. |

---

### Notes & Context
* **Fiscal Year Period:** Texas's fiscal year runs from September 1 through August 31.
* **Source:** State of Texas Annual Cash Report (FY 2026), Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts.
