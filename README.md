# Texas Statutes & Regulations Blocking Echo Lake Incinerator Operation

| Law / Regulation | Exact Citation | What It Requires & Why It Blocks This Facility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Texas Clean Air Act** | Texas Health & Safety Code, Chapter 382 (specifically **§ 382.0518**, Preconstruction Permit) | Prohibits constructing, modifying, or restarting a facility that emits air contaminants without a TCEQ permit proving the use of **Best Available Control Technology (BACT)**. A 1958 brick furnace lacks modern pollution controls. |
| **New Source Review (NSR) Air Permits** | 30 TAC Chapter 116 (Subchapter B, **§ 116.110**) | Requires any unpermitted or long-abandoned emission source to obtain a full air permit before firing up. Because Tarrant County is in the DFW **ozone nonattainment area**, stricter Nonattainment NSR rules (**Subchapter B, Division 5**) require purchasing costly emission offsets. |
| **Small Municipal Waste Combustor Emission Limits** | 30 TAC Chapter 113, Subchapter D, Division 3 (**§§ 113.2100–113.2174**) | Sets strict numeric caps for municipal incinerators burning 35–250 tons/day (this plant was designed for 200 tons/day). Mandates continuous emission monitoring and scrubbers for **dioxins/furans, mercury, lead, cadmium, acid gases, and particulate matter**. |
| **Particulate & Incineration Control Rules** | 30 TAC Chapter 111, Subchapter A (**§§ 111.121–111.129**) | Enforces strict opacity (smoke visibility) limits, minimum combustion temperatures, residence times, and stack testing requirements that the 1950s furnace design cannot meet. |
| **Control of Air Pollution from Nitrogen Compounds** | 30 TAC Chapter 117, Subchapter B / Subchapter D | Caps nitrogen oxide (NOx) emissions specifically for combustion units operating in the **Dallas–Fort Worth eight-hour ozone nonattainment area** (which includes Fort Worth/Tarrant County). |
| **Federal Operating Permits (Title V)** | 30 TAC Chapter 122 | Requires major air emission sources—including municipal waste combustors—to hold an active federal Title V operating permit certifying ongoing compliance with all state and federal air rules. |
| **Texas Solid Waste Disposal Act** | Texas Health & Safety Code, Chapter 361 (specifically **§ 361.061**) | Prohibits operating any municipal solid waste (MSW) processing or incineration facility without an active TCEQ permit, public notice, and local land-use review. |
| **Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) Facility Rules** | 30 TAC Chapter 330 (specifically **§ 330.7**, Permit Required, & **Subchapter M**, Location Restrictions) | Requires a Type V MSW processing permit to burn trash. Subchapter M also imposes **wetland, floodplain, and buffer zone restrictions**—blocking waste operations right on the edge of Echo Lake. |
| **Texas Risk Reduction Program (TRRP)** | 30 TAC Chapter 350 | Governs soil and groundwater contamination. Prevents disturbing, redeveloping, or reusing the site until heavy metals and toxic ash in the unlined pits are tested and remediated to state cleanup standards. |
